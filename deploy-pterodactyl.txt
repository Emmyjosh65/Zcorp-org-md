ZCORP TELEGRAM BOT
PTERODACTYL SETUP
=================

STEP 1
Create your Node.js server in Pterodactyl.

STEP 2
Use Node.js 20 or newer.

STEP 3
Upload these files:

index.js
package.json
.env
.env.example
.gitignore
README.md
deploy-pterodactyl.txt

STEP 4
Open the Pterodactyl console.

STEP 5
Run:

npm install

STEP 6
After installation finishes, run:

npm start

STEP 7
You should see:

🚀 ZCORP Telegram Bot is starting...

STEP 8
Open your Telegram bot.

Send:

/start

STEP 9
The bot should show:

JOIN CHANNEL
JOIN COMMUNITY
I'VE JOINED — CONTINUE

STEP 10
Press:

I'VE JOINED — CONTINUE

The bot will open the ZCORP main menu.

IMPORTANT
The continue button does NOT verify Telegram membership.

GEMINI
Make sure .env contains:

BOT_TOKEN=YOUR_NEW_TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN
GEMINI_API_KEY=YOUR_GEMINI_API_KEY

Never put your real API keys directly inside index.js.

NEVER upload .env to GitHub.
